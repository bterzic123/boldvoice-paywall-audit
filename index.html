What this is

We opened your paywall the way a new user does, and checked it against the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and State of Paywalls 2026 is what we learned from 78,000+ of their paywalls. We installed BoldVoice, went through the paywall and what happens when you close it, read your App Store listing, and compared each part with what top apps do and what wins in tests.

Your paywall already does something most don't: only 24% of trial paywalls show a trial timeline, and yours does, with the charge date on it. So these ideas are about what happens around it. Benchmarks are for Education apps in the US, your largest App Store market. For that slice the typical paywall turns 5.34% of views into trials, and 31.3% of trials into paid subscribers.

We did not redesign your app

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

The report found that changes to the offer win more tests than changes to visuals or copy, so the offer comes first. Each idea links to its screen in the prototype.

01

Give people a second look when they close

Today the X goes straight to the Initial Assessment. The price doesn't come back in that session. A second screen on close can sell the same 12-month plan another way: about €1.92 a week, your 4.8 rating and a review in a learner's own words.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 4% of apps show a second paywall on close. It converts at 0.88× the main paywall and beats it in 45% of apps. See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on Adapty data
02

Put your 4.8 stars on the paywall

Your App Store listing carries a 4.8 from 54K ratings, 2 million+ users and a place among Apple's Top 10 Education apps. The paywall shows none of it. One row above the timeline brings it to the moment people decide.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 27% of top paywalls show social proof. See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning
A/B tests
03

Write the headline to the person

“How your free 7-day trial works” is the same for everyone. If you know their name or why they want to sound clearer before the paywall, put it in the headline, for example “Your 7 days to clearer English start today”.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Only 2% of top paywall headlines use what the app knows about the user. See the screen CR +8–17%Observed in winning
A/B tests
04

Hold the X for three seconds

The close button is there from the first frame, so people can leave before they read the timeline, which is the best thing on the screen. A short delay gives it time to land.
